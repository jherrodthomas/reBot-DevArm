## 1. Purpose

The budget from a demand to standstill for every stop function: detection, chain or controller reaction, drive response, the stopping time and stopping angle per joint at the speed and payload limits of WP-C-01, and the separation distance that follows per ISO 13855 for speed-and-separation operation. The stopping angle the budget assumes is bounded by the end-stop and pinch-point analysis of WP-H-08.

## 2. Response-time budget per function

Table per SF-nn: detection, reaction, drive, stop; target and measured (TBC until WP-V-02).

## 3. Stopping performance

Stopping time and angle per joint, category 0 (coast, with the gripper holding) and category 1.

## 4. Separation distance

ISO 13855 S = K·T + C with the approach speed of WP-C-06 §3; what it means on a desk.

## 5. Open items

Every figure is a target until WP-V-02 measures it.
