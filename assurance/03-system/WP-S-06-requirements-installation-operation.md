## 1. Purpose

What the deploying organisation, the builder and the operator must do: fixturing, the E-stop within reach, the operator position, the workspace, the PSU, the harness build, the E-stop test before every session, maintenance of the 3D-printed covers and the fasteners, decommissioning.

## 2. Installation

Fixturing points and torque, the workbench, the cable route, the PSU and its disconnect.

## 3. Operation

Modes, the pre-session checks, the limits per operating envelope.

## 4. Maintenance

Inspection intervals for covers, fasteners, harness; what a damaged printed part means.

## 5. Decommissioning

Power removal, disposal.
