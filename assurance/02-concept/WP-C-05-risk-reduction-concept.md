## 1. Purpose

For every rated row of WP-C-03: which step of ISO 12100 §6 reduces it (inherently safe design, safeguarding and complementary measures, information for use), which design measure DM-nn, safety function SF-nn or instruction IFU-nn does it, and the residual-risk class that remains. Closes the package's finding that the adequacy-of-risk-reduction record was absent for 18 of 33 hazards (GAP-27).

## 2. Allocation per hazard

One row per rated row of WP-C-03 §5 with the three steps.

## 3. Design measures

DM-nn: chamfers and fillets, joint-gap limits per ISO 13854, covers, the yaw limit block, base fixturing points, harness restraint, the keyed supply connector.

## 4. Information for use

IFU-nn: the limits of WP-C-01, the fixturing and the operator position, the E-stop test before every session, the rest-break and lifting guidance.

## 5. Residual risk

Per row: class and the condition under which it is accepted; feeds WP-K-01 §9.
