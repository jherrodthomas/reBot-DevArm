# 00-assessment

The baseline review of what the fork inherited: the B601-DM hardware record and the concept-phase engineering
package, finding by finding, against the standards of [WP-M-01 §4](../01-management/WP-M-01-assurance-strategy.md#4-the-standards-and-how-each-one-is-used).

| Document | Content |
|---|---|
| [gap-assessment.md](gap-assessment.md) | GAP-01…GAP-36: mechanical, electrical and emergency stop, motion control and software, collaborative operation, process; the map onto the package's gap register; priority actions; the decisions raised |

Every finding becomes a defeater in the [safety case](../10-safety-case/WP-K-01-safety-case.md) and a question in
the [decision register](../01-management/decisions/README.md) where only the lead can settle it. This folder is not
a registered work product; it is the input every work product cites.
