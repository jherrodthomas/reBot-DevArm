## 1. Purpose

The mechanical analysis the risk assessment and the stopping budget rest on: mass and payload moment at reach, the base fixing and the tip-over moment of an unfixtured base, the joint gaps per ISO 13854, the mechanical end-stops and the yaw limit block, the strength of the sheet-metal brackets and of the 3D-printed covers and finger. The maximum stopping angle per joint is an input to the stopping budget of WP-S-04.

## 2. Mass, payload and fixing

The 4.5 kg arm with 1.5 kg at 650 mm; the fixing points and torque.

## 3. Pinch and crush points

Joint by joint against ISO 13854 minimum gaps; the gripper jaws.

## 4. End-stops and limits

Per joint: mechanical end-stop, software limit, the margin.

## 5. Printed and sheet-metal parts

Material, load case, margin; what substitution means (AOU-03).
