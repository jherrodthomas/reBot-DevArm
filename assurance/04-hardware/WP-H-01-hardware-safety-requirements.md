## 1. Purpose

Hardware safety requirements HWSR-nn derived from WP-S-02 and WP-S-03: the safety relay and its inputs, the contactor on the 24 V motor supply with mirror contact, the E-stop devices, the enabling device for teach mode, the supply disconnect and fusing, the gripper holding means, the harness and connectors, the joint end-stops and covers.

## 2. Requirements

HWSR-nn per component.

## 3. Environmental and electrical requirements

Temperature, supply tolerance, EMC, the 24 V SELV bus.

## 4. Traceability

To WP-S-02 and WP-S-03; verified by WP-H-06.
