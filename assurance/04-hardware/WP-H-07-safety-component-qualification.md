## 1. Purpose

Per safety component: certificate, well-tried argument or fault exclusion, and the conditions of use the data depends on. The actuators are not safety components; what is claimed of their brake or holding behaviour is recorded here.

## 2. Components

Relay, contactor, E-stop devices, enabling device, the drives' enable path, the gripper holding means.

## 3. Evidence

Certificates and datasheets stored or referenced.

## 4. Open items
