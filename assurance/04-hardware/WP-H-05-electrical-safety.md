## 1. Purpose

Electrical safety of the installation: the mains-fed 24 V PSU (MEAN WELL LRS-350-24 in the upstream BOM), a lockable supply disconnecting device, overcurrent protection per branch, the XT30 harness connectors and the supply input connector, bonding, the SELV argument.

## 2. Supply disconnection

Where the lockable disconnect sits (GAP-17).

## 3. Overcurrent protection

Per branch sizing against the 14.6 A supply and the motor stall currents.

## 4. Connectors and insulation

The supply input (keyed, not a barrel jack), the XT30 chain, strain relief at the joints.

## 5. Verification

EN 60204-1 §18 tests to run in WP-H-06.
