## Assessment

G0 does not meet its exit criteria today, and two of them cannot be met by work alone: the verification reviewer and
QA roles need a second person, and the confirmation reviews need an assessor or an exception to WP-M-02 §10. What
passing G0 would and would not mean:

- **It would mean** the governance plans are in force and the project formally enters P1 Concept, where most of the
  record's work already is (WP-C-01 to C-06 are Drafts).
- **It would not mean** anything about the arm: no safety function exists, no claim is accepted, and the hold point of
  [WP-V-07](../../06-validation/WP-V-07-test-operations.md) still forbids any powered test with a person in reach.

The question for the lead (DQ-023) offers three routes. The recommended one: before the outcome is recorded, the lead
self-reviews and approves at I0 the six plans the gate rests on (WP-M-01, M-02, M-06, M-11, WP-P-01, WP-P-02), branch
protection is set on `main`, and the deferral of CR-01 and CR-03 to G1 is recorded as an exception; G0 then passes
with the conditions below.
