## 1. Purpose

Validation of whatever collaborative claim the lead makes (DQ-005): separation distances for speed-and-separation operation, and, only if a power-and-force-limiting claim is made, the force and pressure measurement per ISO/TS 15066 Annex A on a body-region fixture. Separation distances are those of WP-S-04 at the stopping performance measured in WP-V-02.

## 2. Scope

Which collaborative mode is claimed and which is excluded.

## 3. Separation distance tests

Set-up, measurement, criterion.

## 4. Force and pressure measurement

Only with a PFL claim: fixture, body regions, thresholds.

Results.
