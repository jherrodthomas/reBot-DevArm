## 1. Purpose

Measurement of the stopping time and stopping angle of every joint from the speed and payload limits, for category 0 and category 1 stops; the software joint limits against the mechanical end-stops; the gripper and joint holding on power loss. The measured stopping time and angle feed the separation distances validated in WP-V-03.

## 2. Test specification

Per joint and stop category: set-up, speed, payload, measurement, criterion.

## 3. Holding tests

Gripper holding on power loss; joint behaviour on power loss under payload.

## 4. Report

Measured values against the budget of WP-S-04.
