## 1. Purpose

How a work product or code change is reviewed before it leaves Draft: review types, independence levels I0–I3, entry and exit criteria, checklists, the review record template, and the rule that an AI-drafted pre-review gives no credit above I0.

## 2. Review types

Verification review, walkthrough, inspection; confirmation measures are WP-M-06.

## 3. Independence levels

I0–I3 as in WP-M-06 §3.

## 4. Entry and exit criteria

What a reviewable work product has; what closes a review.

## 5. Checklists

Per work product family.

## 6. Review record template

Identifier, object, reviewer and independence, date, method, findings, outcome; used for confirmation records too.
