## 1. Purpose

How problems and safety anomalies are raised (the issue templates), classified (A to D by worst credible outcome), contained, analysed, corrected and closed; the root-cause categories; who may lift a test stop.

## 2. Intake and triage

Templates, labels, the one-working-day triage of a safety anomaly.

## 3. Classification

Severity classes and root-cause categories.

## 4. Resolution and closure

Containment, root cause, corrective action, verification, closure by someone other than the analyst for class A and B.
